Pokémon cries in assets/capipimon/sounds/cries/

What they are: the Pokémon's cries from the Pokémon games. Pokémon and its sounds are (c) Nintendo,
Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc. Capipimon is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Where they come from:
- Most: Cobblemon (https://gitlab.com/cable-mc/cobblemon), common/src/main/resources/assets/cobblemon/
  sounds/pokemon/<species>/<species>_cry.ogg at commit 99e0a26d7bd5b0f217aacc41da36a2f0964f3b28,
  copied byte for byte and renamed to <species>.ogg.
- A few species Cobblemon only has a silent placeholder for: CobbleCats 1.6 (MIT,
  https://modrinth.com/datapack/cobble-cats) and MundialMons (MIT, https://modrinth.com/project/mundialmons,
  licence text in mundialmons.txt), same file layout.
- cobblemon-cries-sources.txt lists which species came from where, and the species with no real cry
  found (none is shipped for them).

Terms: no licence file covers these sounds. Cobblemon's code is MPL-2.0 and its models are
CC BY-NC 3.0 (cobblemon-models.txt), but neither licence names the cries, which the Cobblemon team did
not author. The add-on packs' MIT licences cover their own work, not Nintendo's audio. They are
included by the project owner's decision, as other Cobblemon servers ship them, in a mod that is not
sold, paywalled or monetised. Remove them if a rights holder asks.
