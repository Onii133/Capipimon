Orbital Laser by Stink-O
https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser (commit 7853371cd2fa6ead2193ba9a2821d182aca6f000)

Used for: techniques in the Capipimon move effects pass (mods/capipimon-models/api/src/fx.rs):
rebuilding world position from the depth buffer, and drawing beams as the distance from each
pixel's view ray to the beam's axis. The effects code is our own; no source files are copied.
