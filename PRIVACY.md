## Privacy

### What Capipimon Launcher does with your Microsoft account

If you play through the Horizon launcher instead, that launcher's own terms and privacy statement apply to it.


- You sign in on Microsoft's own page (microsoft.com/link). Capipimon never sees your password.
- The launcher asks Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang for your Minecraft profile (name, UUID, skin), and checks that
  you own Minecraft: Java Edition.
- Your sign-in tokens stay on your own computer, in Windows' credential store. They are never sent to Capipimon.
- The launcher doesn't read your Xbox friends, activity, stats or other Xbox data, even though Microsoft's
  permission screen lists them.

### What we collect

- **The launcher** collects and sends no data: no statistics, crash reports, pings or advertising IDs.
- **Capipimon's servers** keep only what the game needs to work:
  - your Minecraft name and UUID,
  - your Capipimon progress (creatures, items, quests, settings),
  - chat and server logs, kept for moderation.
- We don't sell or share this data with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

### Your choices

Email the contact address above to get a copy of your data or have it deleted.
To revoke the launcher's access to your Microsoft account, go to https://microsoft.com/consent.

### Children

Capipimon is meant for all ages and asks for no personal information beyond the Minecraft profile above.
