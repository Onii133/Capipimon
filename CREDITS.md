# Credits

Capipimon bundles models, animations, textures and data made by other people. Each part stays under its
author's licence. The licence files live in [`content/licences/`](content/licences/), and the content mod's own
notes are in [`content/CREDITS.md`](content/CREDITS.md) and [`content/CREDITS-ADDONS.md`](content/CREDITS-ADDONS.md).

**Where the files are:** every model and animation sits in `content/cobblemon-models/`, with textures under
`content/assets/capipimon/textures/`. They are separate data files that the mod reads while it runs: none are
compiled into the native mod's program.

**Not monetised.** Several packs are CC BY-NC (non-commercial). Capipimon and these models aren't sold, paywalled
or monetised. Files under CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-SA, MPL-2.0 or GPL-3.0-only keep those licences, and any copy or
change of them must be shared under the same licence.

## Changes to Cobblemon textures (adaptations)

The palette variants `<species>_p3.png`, `<species>_p4.png` and `<species>_p5.png` in `content/assets/capipimon/textures/entity/` are **modified versions** of Cobblemon textures: recolours (palette remaps) of the original textures of Blastoise, Buneary, Lopunny, Lugia, Marshtomp, Mudkip, Pichu, Raichu, Squirtle, Swampert, Wartortle and Zapdos, 36 files in all. Only the colours were changed, by the Capippalist Trade Guild for Capipimon.

Original work: the Cobblemon team, https://gitlab.com/cable-mc/cobblemon, under CC BY-NC 3.0 Unported (full text: `content/licences/cobblemon-models.txt`; in the packaged files it is `licences/cobblemon-models.txt`, in the folder beside the `credits/` folder that holds this file). The recolours are shared under the same licence, are non-commercial, and are not made or endorsed by the Cobblemon team. Other changes to Cobblemon files are listed in `content/CREDITS.md` (in the packaged files: `credits/credits-changes.md`).

## Talent NPC skins

Talent NPCs wear the skins of the Minecraft accounts listed in `native/api/data/npc_skins.json`, loaded from Mojang's
skin servers. Skins remain their owners'. Pipkin Pippa's skin is by MitchieSoren.

## Licence texts

Packs that shipped no licence file have one in `content/licences/`: a first line naming the licence the pack
declares on Modrinth, then that licence's full official text. In the MIT copies, the copyright line names the
pack's owner on Modrinth and the year it was first published there.
